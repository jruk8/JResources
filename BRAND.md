# JManhunt brand terms

Files in this repo are available under CC BY 4.0 (see LICENSE).
For brand uses (the JManhunt name, logo, and brand art), these
terms add conditions on that permission.

## Always allowed

- Fan and community use: videos, streams, thumbnails, wiki images.
- Server use: banners, listings, and pages saying you run JManhunt.
- Monetized channels: the above are fine even when monetized.

## Not allowed without permission

- Merchandise: no shirts, stickers, or other goods carrying the
  brand without prior written permission.
- Endorsement confusion: nothing that implies official status,
  affiliation, or approval.
- Modified marks: do not alter the logo (colors, proportions,
  elements) or present altered versions as official.
- Product identity: do not use the name or logo as the identity
  of another product, server network, or company.

## Forks

- Forks of JManhunt must rebrand with their own name and logo.
- Text credit such as "based on JManhunt" or "a fork of JManhunt"
  is fine, without using the logo as your identity.

## Credit

- Credit "JManhunt by jruk" with a link to
  https://github.com/jruk8/JManhunt where reasonable.

## Misuse

- Uses that break these terms fall outside the permission
  granted here.
